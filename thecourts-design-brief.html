The brief

What we’re building & why

The Courts Loyalty Programme launches 1 June 2026. Members earn points across all 7 tenants and redeem rewards across the ecosystem. The on-ground design is the physical marketing engine that drives sign-ups and makes the programme feel real.

1

🎯 The programme in one line

RM1 spent at any tenant = 1 point. 200 points gifted on sign-up so members can redeem something immediately. 5 reward tiers from a free espresso to premium multi-tenant experiences. QR code to join on every piece.

2

📣 What the design must communicate

Three things only: (1) There’s a loyalty programme here. (2) Sign up and get 200 points free right now. (3) Here’s where to scan. Everything else is secondary. If a piece tries to say more than three things, it says nothing.

3

⚡ The welcome boost is the headline

“200 points. Yours today. Free.” That’s the hero message on most pieces. It maps to Tier 1 rewards immediately — a free espresso at Tanamerah, RM5 off a PSC court session. The member feels like they won on Day 1.

4

📱 Everything gets a digital twin

Design print first. Then each file gets adapted for Instagram Story (9:16) and feed post (1:1). Same visual language, different crop. This means design decisions made in print must hold up digitally too — no tiny text, no overloaded layouts.

Visual direction

How it should look & feel

Aligned with The Courts’ brand identity from their existing content direction. Premium but warm. Editorial. Not a gym flyer. Not corporate. Closer to Soho House than a membership card rack.

Cream is the base. Black and gold do the heavy lifting. Forest green for accents and earn mechanics. No bright colours, no gradients.

Typography

Playfair Display
Headlines, hero text, tier names — bold serif, editorial weight
DM Sans — Body copy, descriptions
Clean, warm sans-serif for all body text and labels
DM MONO — LABELS · CODES · DATA
Monospace for point values, earn rates, QR callouts

Tone & mood

Premium Warm Inviting Community Confident Not salesy

Think Soho House meets Nuanu Bali. The loyalty programme is not a discount scheme — it’s access to a lifestyle ecosystem. The design should feel like a privilege, not a promotion. References: The Courts’ own content direction, clean wellness brand aesthetics.

Photography & imagery direction

  • ✓ Real candid shots from The Courts — no stock photography
  • ✓ Natural light, movement in real moments, warm interiors
  • ✓ Shadows, texture, environmental storytelling
  • ✗ No heavy graphic treatments, no neon overlays, no stock smiles
  • ✗ Avoid over-designed loyalty programme clichés (stars, coins, flashy badges)
Online crossover

Print → digital, built in from the start

Every print piece has a digital twin. Design the print version first, then adapt the same file — same visual system, different crop and format. This avoids two separate design rounds and keeps the brand consistent online and on-ground.

Execution order

What to build first

If budget or time is tight, this is the sequence. Priority 1 pieces must be done before 1 June launch. Priority 2 within the first two weeks of June. Priority 3 by end of June.

    1
    🪧 Counter tent cards — all 7 tenants
    7 unique versions, A5 landscape double-sided. Highest conversion piece. Where the transaction happens. Needs to be live on Day 1 at every desk.
    2
    📺 LED standee content — entrance
    9:16 digital format, designed to loop on the LED standee at entrance. Same file exports as A0/A1 print fallback for the common area. The brand moment everyone walks past.
    3
    🏆 Tier catalogue wall poster
    A2/A3, highly visual. Answers “what do I get?” Works as the Instagram carousel too — earns double duty across print and digital.
    4
    🎾 PSC court divider A-frame
    Placed at the court divider — visible during play and from the spectator/waiting area. Highest volume venue, best acquisition point.
    5
    🍹 NOA & ☕ Tanamerah table talkers
    High dwell time. People are sitting, eating, waiting. Best environment for slightly longer engagement with the programme mechanics.
    6
    🪞 Everything else
    Mirror clings, schedule strips, coaster cards, Padelground A-frame, 176 Avenue poster. All high-quality but lower urgency for launch day.

Non-negotiables for every piece

  • QR code on every single piece — large enough to scan without picking it up. Test it from 50cm away.
  • Sign-up URL printed below every QR code as backup text
  • 200-pt welcome boost called out wherever there is space
  • RM1 = 1 point earn rate stated on any piece longer than A6
  • All 7 tenant logos somewhere on the hero poster and tier catalogue — shows the scope of the programme
  • Design print-first but export digital twins from the same file, same session
  • Supply print-ready files as PDF/X-1a and separate digital files as PNG/JPG at 72dpi
For Dillion

Questions to answer in this session

These are the decisions we need to make together today before any design work starts.

1 🎨 — Do you have The Courts brand assets?

Logo files (SVG or high-res PNG), any existing brand guidelines or colour codes, existing marketing materials to reference. We need to make sure the loyalty programme design sits within the same visual world as everything else they’ve already produced.

2 🖨️ — Print vendor — do you have a contact?

We need a printer who can handle large format (A0), standard (A5/A6), and specialty (coaster cards, mirror clings, A-frames). If not, we need to find one this week so files are ready to go before launch.

3 🪧 — Counter tent cards — one master or 7 unique?

My recommendation: one master layout, 7 colour-coded or tenant-named versions with the specific voucher swapped in. The hero message stays the same, the earn mechanic stays the same, only the specific redemption offer changes per tenant.

4 📸 — Photography — what do we have?

The interns’ content brief calls for real candid shots, no stock. What photography assets does The Courts already have? If we’re short, do we plan a quick shoot before launch, or do we need to work with what exists now?

5 ⏰ — Timeline — what’s realistic?

Launch is 1 June. Priority 1 pieces need to be at the printer at least 5 working days before that. That means final files by 23 May at the latest for launch-day pieces. What’s your capacity between now and then?

6 📱 — Digital twin workflow

For each print piece, we need a 9:16 Story version and a 1:1 feed version. Are you designing in a tool that makes this easy to re-export (Figma, Illustrator, Canva)? And who handles the social posting — the interns or you?
