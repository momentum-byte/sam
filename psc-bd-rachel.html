Working Together
Our weekly rhythm — how it works for you
📅 Monday — BD Sync (1 hour)
  • 📋Rachel shares pipeline update — what closed last week, what's pending, what needs Sam's input
  • 🧭Sam shares what's coming in from outbound — so Rachel knows what inbound to expect
  • 🔄Senior BD shares outbound pipeline — any warm leads that might convert to inbound Rachel should know about
  • ⚡Blockers surfaced and resolved together — not saved for Slack messages
  • 🎯Priority focus for the week agreed — everyone leaves knowing what matters most
⚡ Thursday — Stand-Up (15 mins)
  • 🟢What moved since Monday — quick wins called out
  • 🔴What's stuck — flag anything that hasn't moved and needs a nudge
  • 📬Any new inbound that came in mid-week worth flagging to the team
  • 🗓️Preview of the week ahead — anything coming up Sam should know about
What Sam commits to in every sync
  • ✅Start on time and respect your 20-hour commitment — no expectation to attend calls outside agreed hours
  • ✅Give you clear SLAs — you should never have to guess how fast to respond or how long to chase a lead
  • ✅Surface the gate status on the 1st of every month — you know whether full commission is unlocked before you start
  • ✅Attribute commission correctly — if you closed it, you get paid for it. No disputes.
  • ✅Monthly commission paid on the 10th — clear statement shared before payment so you can verify
How Rachel can make syncs work better
  • 💡Send a quick pipeline snapshot to Sam before Monday sync — even a voice note — so the call is a discussion, not a download
  • 💡Flag when you're at capacity — if the inbound volume is too high for 20 hours, say it in the Monday call, not after the fact
  • 💡Mark leads as won/lost/dormant in the tracker weekly — this protects your commission attribution and keeps the pipeline honest
  • 💡If something about a client feels off — escalate to Sam early, not when it becomes a problem
