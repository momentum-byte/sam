Why This Audience is Rare

The KLGCC address self-selects. Players here are professionals, business owners, and executives from the Bukit Kiara, Mont Kiara, Hartamas, and Damansara belt. They pay premium court fees willingly, visit multiple times a week, and actively share their sessions on social media.

The core 25–45 demographic (73% of audience) represents KL's highest-spending, most brand-responsive cohort. For financial products, premium automotive, wellness, and lifestyle brands — this is the audience that cannot be efficiently reached through mass media.
