01 · Where I am

The idea, in 90 seconds

GOL starts at the forgotten concrete courts inside PPR flats across the Klang Valley. Sport brings youth to the court; the court becomes the doorway to skills, mentorship and opportunity they can't currently reach.

But I'm deliberately not raising or building yet. An advisor challenged me that I was solution-first — so first, a six-week ground validation sprint: a small working group living inside three PPR communities, testing whether the problem is real, felt, and unserved. If the evidence says no, GOL doesn't happen. That's the point.
