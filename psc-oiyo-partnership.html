Who We Reach

TARGET AUDIENCE

Three interconnected audience groups — each valuable to both PSC and OiYO — united by active, aspirational, family-first lifestyles in urban KL.

👧
THE LITTLE PICKLERS
Ages 4–15 · Primary Programme Participant
Expat Kids Affluent Malaysian Japanese · Korean American · Russian Learning Fresh Students

Motor-skills-ready, social-play hungry. Already attending enrichment programmes. First exposure to racket sports. High lifetime value if retained through junior pathway. OiYO's core product user — the child carries, uses, and shows off the bottle daily.

👨‍👩‍👧
THE ACTIVE PARENTS
25–45 · Decision Maker & Wallet
HHI RM10K+ Dual Income School-Run Routine Instagram-Active Wellness Conscious

OiYO's primary buyer. PSC's primary enrolling customer. They value quality, aesthetics, and convenience. They share milestones on social media. They buy when they trust — and trust is built courtside. Saturday family sessions convert parents into repeat buyers and community evangelists.

🏢
CORPORATE FAMILIES
HR · Wellness Leads · Team Buyers
MNCs in KL Family Day Budgets Employee Benefits HRDC Adjacent Bulk Gifting

Companies seek meaningful family wellness benefits. PSC + OiYO co-branded "Corporate Family Pack" (court session + junior enrolment + OiYO kit) is a differentiated, premium offering. Strong channel for bulk OiYO sales and PSC event bookings simultaneously.

The Exchange of Value

WHAT EACH PARTNER WINS

This only works long-term if both sides walk away with more than they put in. Here's the honest case for each brand.

PICKLE SOCIAL CLUB
Sport · Community · Experience Platform
Recurring Cash Revenue
RM2,000/month naming fee + programme fees + revenue share = RM367K+ annual projection. Real, stackable money — not just logo swaps.
Off-Peak Court Utilisation
Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings converted from idle to earning. Junior programmes fill the hours adult casual play won't.
Differentiated Brand Story
PSC becomes KL's leading junior sport development platform — not just an adult pickleball club. Changes how schools, parents, and media perceive us.
Access to OiYO's Family Audience
OiYO's Shopee (33K followers), mall presence, and influencer network introduces PSC to families who haven't yet found us through sport channels.
Content & Credibility Engine
240+ co-branded touchpoints per year. Kids with OiYO bottles and PSC paddles are the most shareable content in the family lifestyle space.
OiYO by MONTIGO
Family Drinkware · Mealtime · Lifestyle Brand
In-Use Brand Salience — Not Shelf Salience
OiYO bottles used on court every session. Kids carry them to school, to practice, to parties. That's daily brand exposure money can't replicate on Shopee.
Premium Family Audience Access
Expat and affluent Malaysian parents at PSC are OiYO's exact target customer — high HHI, aesthetics-conscious, willing to pay for quality kids gear.
Retail Conversion — Beyond Mall Footfall
On-site Pro Shop sales, PSC promo codes, and camp bundles give OiYO a sales channel outside their existing retail footprint. Measurable sell-through.
Sport + Health Brand Story
"OiYO champions Small Wins" comes alive when kids celebrate their first rally with an OiYO bottle in hand. Authentic storytelling that ads alone can't create.
R&D Feedback Loop
Semi-annual product testing surveys at PSC give OiYO live feedback from active kids and parents on product performance, colours, and features.
