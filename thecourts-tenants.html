How It Works

Simple to Earn. Rewarding to Redeem. 🏆

1

🛒 Spend & Earn

Every RM1 spent at The Courts = 1 point. Works across every tenant — book a Reformer class at RM110, earn 110 pts on the spot.

2

📈 Points Accumulate

Points build up with every visit to The Courts. The closer members get to a tier reward, the more motivated they are to return — to your studio.

3

🎁 Redeem at Milestones

At each tier, rewards unlock. The first milestone is easy to hit by design — so members feel the value of the programme from their very first few visits.

🔒
Your data stays yours. Cross-tenant data is never shared between tenants. The Courts shares only portfolio-level trends — total redemptions, demographics, peak periods — on a quarterly basis.
