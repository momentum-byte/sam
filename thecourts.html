About The Courts

The Courts at KLGCC Resort is KL's premier multi-sport and lifestyle destination, nestled within the prestigious KLGCC golf resort in Bukit Kiara. More than a venue — it's a thriving community of athletes, wellness seekers, and lifestyle enthusiasts from Damansara Heights, Bangsar, Mont Kiara, and Bukit Tunku.


Operated by Momentum (Thud Thud Sdn. Bhd.), The Courts brings together best-in-class tenants under one roof, creating a uniquely powerful platform for brands to activate, engage, and grow alongside an affluent, active audience.

"Where purpose meets comfort — and every aspiration finds its home."
