The Why
🎯 Purpose of This Framework

PSC's BD team is small and lean. To drive RM759,000 in revenue this year, every person on the team needs to be commercially motivated — not just operationally busy. This framework proposes a commission structure that is effort-weighted, revenue-linked, and fair to the business.

  • 💡Commission only pays when revenue lands — zero risk to PSC if deals don't close
  • 💡Outbound rates are higher than inbound — effort is rewarded proportionally
  • 💡Total commission cost stays well below 10% of revenue generated — highly efficient
  • 💡Team bonus drives collaboration — prevents silo mentality between inbound and outbound
  • 💡Sam signs off monthly — Mark approves structure once, not every deal

Performance Uplift
🚀 Tier Accelerators

Tiers kick in when monthly revenue crosses a threshold. The tier rate replaces the base commission rate on that revenue category — it does not stack. This protects PSC's margin while meaningfully rewarding high performance.

Rachel — Inbound Revenue Tiers Part-time

Monthly Inbound Revenue Rate Uplift
Up to RM20,000 Base rates: 2.5% court bookings · RM50 flat small events · 3% larger events / corporate / clinics
RM20,001 – RM35,000 +0.5% uplift on all inbound lines (e.g. court bookings → 3%, larger events → 3.5%)
RM35,001 – RM50,000 +1% uplift on all inbound lines (e.g. court bookings → 3.5%, larger events → 4%)
Above RM50,000 ⭐ +1.5% uplift on all inbound lines (e.g. court bookings → 4%, larger events → 4.5%)

Thresholds scaled to part-time capacity. Hitting RM50K+ inbound as a 20-hr/week exec is exceptional — deserves exceptional reward.

Closing Reward
🏅 Conversion Bonus

This is not an activity bonus — it rewards actual closed deals, not meetings booked or proposals sent. No closing, no bonus. Simple and honest.

Person Threshold Bonus Rationale
Rachel ≥ 8 deals closed in a month (any revenue line) RM300 Scaled to part-time capacity — 8 closes is strong for 20 hrs/week
Senior BD ≥ 5 outbound deals closed in a month RM500 Outbound-only threshold — rewards hunting, not just inbound volume
📌 A "closed deal" = signed agreement, confirmed booking, or purchase order received. Not a verbal commitment or proposal stage.
Collaboration Layer
🤝 Team Bonus

Individual commission drives personal performance. The team bonus drives collaboration. When the team hits the monthly revenue target together — everyone wins. This prevents silo mentality between inbound and outbound lanes.

Monthly Team Revenue Target: RM50,000 (PSC BD-attributable revenue across all lines)

If target is hit → team bonus paid:
  · Rachel: RM300
  · Senior BD: RM500

Sam signs off monthly. Mark approves the structure once — not every deal or every month.
📌 Why this works: Rachel qualifying a strong lead for Senior BD to close benefits them both. Senior BD's outbound deal that brings a corporate back inbound next month benefits Rachel too. The team bonus makes that collaboration financially visible.

Payout Structure
📅 Monthly Payout — Why Not Quarterly

The recommendation is monthly payout, cash-received basis. Here's why monthly wins over quarterly for this team.

✅ Why Monthly

  • 💰Motivates immediately — effort and reward connect in the same month
  • 💰Essential for Rachel at RM3K base — quarterly is too long to wait for meaningful uplift
  • 💰Easier to track — one month, one clean reconciliation
  • 💰Cash-received trigger means PSC never pays before money lands — zero finance risk

❌ Why Not Quarterly

  • ⚠️Demotivating — team closes in June, gets paid in September
  • ⚠️Creates disputes if Senior BD leaves before quarter ends
  • ⚠️Harder to reconcile — 3 months of deals in one calculation
  • ⚠️Rachel's financial motivation suffers most — part-time staff need timely reward
Monthly Payout Process
  • 📋Sam submits commission statement to Mark by 5th of every month — deals closed, cash received, commission due per person
  • ✅Mark reviews and approves within 3 working days
  • 💳Commission paid on 10th of every month — clean processing window for finance

Governance
⚖️ Design Guardrails
  • ✅Commission cost should stay 3–8% of total revenue at on-target performance — all simulations above fall within this range
  • ✅Commission only triggers on cash received — PSC never pays commission on deals that don't settle
  • ✅Tier accelerator replaces base rate — never stacks — protects margin at high performance months
  • ✅Rachel's outbound kicker (2%) requires first contact logged before anyone else touches the lead — prevents attribution disputes
  • ✅Sam signs off monthly commission payout — Mark approves structure once, not every deal
  • ⚠️Brand Partnership deals (9%) on large one-off deals can create outlier months — consider 50% on signing / 50% on delivery for deals above RM30,000
  • ⚠️Set a minimum deal size of RM1,500 for Corporate/Brand commission to apply — prevents gaming with micro-deals
