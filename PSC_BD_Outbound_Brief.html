Market Structure
The 3 Commercial Pillars
Every lead you ever touch falls into one of these. Know which you're in before you open your mouth.
Pillar 1 · Highest Priority

Corporate & Team Building

Who buys: HR leads, People & Culture teams, Team Leads, Office Managers, Employer Branding.

Why they buy: They need bonding, engagement, low-pressure inclusiveness, wellness, morale. They want "something employees actually enjoy."

Their real pain: Dinners are boring. Offsites are expensive. Gym challenges exclude people. They need something that creates natural interaction AND shareable moments — for 10, 30, or 100 pax.

Brand Activation & Partnerships

Who buys: Brand managers, marketing teams, experiential agencies, lifestyle/creator brands, community managers.

Why they buy: Cultural relevance, content, attention, participation, "cool factor," shareability. They want earned media and consumer touchpoints.

Their real pain: Traditional activations feel stale. They want something hands-on that people will post about. PSC provides the captive audience + organic content environment.

Premium Pricing Content-driven Campaign Windows

Who buys: Friend groups, birthday organisers, couples, bridal/bachelor groups, family organisers, social communities.

Why they buy: Celebration, novelty, effortless planning, premium fun, photos, inclusiveness. They want something unique vs a bar or restaurant.

Sales Readiness
Objection Handling — Every One You'll Hear
Memorise these. Every objection has been anticipated. There is no good reason to lose a deal on these.
What They Say Why They're Really Saying It Your Response
"We've never played pickleball." Fear of looking incompetent in front of colleagues "Perfect — we teach the whole group in 5 minutes. It's designed for people who've never played. Social-first, gentle pace."
"It's not sporty enough / too sporty." Unclear what it actually feels like "It's social-first — think of it as an interactive social experience with play woven in. Not a fitness class. Not a tournament. A hosted gathering that happens to use paddles."
"We don't know our headcount yet." Decision isn't made internally yet "No problem — we do range pricing. Hold a date with no commitment, confirm pax 7 days before. Our formats work from 10 to 100."
"We usually just do team dinners." Comfortable with the familiar "Dinners are great for eating, but people go home without having actually interacted. PSC gives you the dinner AND the bonding — add F&B and you've got both."
"Our budget is tight." Comparing to wrong benchmarks "Compared to a team dinner for 20 pax at a restaurant, our Corporate Rally works out cheaper per head — and you get an actual activity, a host, and photos they'll post."
"We're not sure about the date." Low urgency / committee required "1-click hold — no deposit, no commitment, just locks the slot. Easy to release if it doesn't work. Weekends especially fill up fast."
"What ROI does this give our brand?" Need to justify to a budget holder "We can provide a metrics pack — attendee photos, IG story reach, content pieces. Add our content capture team and you leave with a full content library."
"We need to think about it." No clear next step / not enough urgency "Totally fine. The slot closes on [date] — I'll send the one-pager now and check back in 5 days. If you want, I'll hold a provisional slot in the meantime."

Discovery Call — What to Ask
  • What's the objective / occasion?
  • Who's the audience (team, new hires, brand guests)?
  • Preferred date and time slot?
  • Pax range (min–max)?
  • Dietary requirements?
  • Branding / photo policy?
  • What does success look like to you?
  • What's your budget range?
Reduce Friction — Do These Always
  • Transparent packages (no hidden costs)
  • 1-click date hold — no deposit to lock
  • Clear max/min pax in every proposal
  • Simple payment terms in contract
  • Range pricing for headcount uncertainty
  • Pre-written ROS (run-of-show) ready to share
  • One-pager leave-behind always on hand

Post-Contact Cadence
The Follow-Up Sequence
Most deals die not because of "no" — they die because of silence. Follow up every time, on schedule.

CORPORATE OUTBOUND SEQUENCE ↓

D1Send

First outbound + one-pager

Cold outreach with role-based hook. Attach one-pager (hero image, 3 offers + pricing, FAQ, CTA). Subject: "Team reset in 2 hours — zero experience needed."

D5Value

Gallery bump

Send photo gallery or event highlight. Don't ask for anything — just show them what it looks like. "Thought you'd enjoy seeing last week's corporate rally."

D12Scarcity

Date scarcity close

Specific slot closing. "Our weekday mornings in [month] are filling up — wanted to flag this before it goes." Offer 1-click date hold.

Q+1Reactivate

Quarterly reactivation with case study

New quarter, new reason to engage. Share a case study or NPS quote from a recent client. "Q4 is a great time to lock in your year-end team experience."

BRAND ACTIVATION SEQUENCE ↓

D1Deck

Deck + brand brief request

Send activation deck. Ask them to share their brief or upcoming campaign window.

D5Ideas

Creative concept follow-up

Send 2–3 concept ideas tailored to their brand. Show initiative — don't wait for their brief.

D12Window

Campaign window hold

Flag a specific activation window. "We have [date range] available — ideal for a campaign in [month]."

PRIVATE / BIRTHDAY SEQUENCE ↓

D1Options

Package options

Share the Birthday Social Package or P&P packages with pricing. Keep it simple — 2–3 options max.

D5Bump

Simple bump

"Just wanted to check if you had any questions — happy to hold a date for you."

D12Last call

Last call + urgency

Specific date or weekend slot flagged as filling. "Wanted to check — [date] is still available but going fast."

Post-Event Engine
The Retention Sequence — After Every Event
The event is not the end of the deal. It's the start of the next one. Run this sequence after every single booking.
24h

Thank you + gallery link + NPS survey + referral offer. Send the photo gallery. Ask: "Who else in your company or social circle should experience this?" Referral: give RM100, get RM100 credit.

7 days

Sunset Social Club invite + WhatsApp community opt-in. "We'd love to have you back — here's our weekly social. Join the community." Begin the 101 Beginner Pathway for non-members.

14 days

Segment-specific rebook offer. Corp: "How about Q4? We can reserve the same slot." Private: "Birthday club — get priority access every year." Brand: "Recurring activation proposal ready."

30 days

Membership and league upsell. 3-pack, 6-pack, 12-pack discounts. Corporate league or quarterly package. Monthly social nights. Private lessons optional pathway.

Ongoing

WhatsApp alumni community + monthly social nights + loyalty rewards + early access. Keep them warm between events. Community = the retention engine.
