🏟️ Venue Commission Framework

For any outbound client Sam originates who takes up PSC courts, coaches, or non-venue add-ons — the following commission structure applies. PSC always invoices the client directly. Sam facilitates on PSC's behalf.

Commission Structure
Category Item Sam's Commission
Venue Court Rental 10% of rate card price
Venue Coach / Facilitator 10% of rate card price
Non-Venue Custom Paddle / Printed Jersey 10% of marked-up price (max 20% markup, split 10/10 with PSC)
Discount Rules — Protecting Both Sides ⚖️
Situation Who Decides Sam's Commission Basis
Standard rate card booking — ✅ Original rate card price
PSC published bulk discount used as sales tool
5+ dates / 10+ hours → 10% off  |  10+ dates / 20+ hours → 20% off		 Sam (within rate card) ✅ Original rate card price — PSC priced this in
PSC / Mark offers one-off special discount Mark ✅ Original rate card price — Sam not penalised for Mark's call
Deal too good to lose — client needs a bit more % beyond rate card Joint — Sam proposes, Mark approves ❌ Discounted rate — both accepted the trade-off together
📌 The Golden Rule: Sam cannot offer any discount beyond the published bulk discount tiers without Mark's prior approval. If both agree the deal is worth it, commission follows the discounted rate. No surprises for either side.
