01 / 03 — The Concept

The
Courts
Loyalty
Program

One membership. Seven tenants. A marketing engine that turns visits into data, data into campaigns, and campaigns into a community.

1
The Play

A Shared Ecosystem

One card works across all 7 tenants. Members earn everywhere — PSC, NOA, TRIBE, Flow Studio, 176 Avenue, Padelground, Tanamerah. The Courts owns the full picture. Tenants benefit from each other's traffic.

2
The Engine

A Marketing Machine

Points drive visits, not cashback. The programme tells us who's coming, who's gone cold, and where to cross-sell. Every quarter, The Courts uses this intelligence to run campaigns no single tenant could do alone.

3
The Approach

Launch Fast, Scale Smart

Start with the basics done well. Get members earning, get tenants seeing redemptions in Week 1. Advanced automation and tier upgrades follow once we've proven the model with real data.

The Voucher Principle

All vouchers are conditional — not open cash. Disguised as value offers, they drive higher spend. The Courts defines the yumminess. Tenants define what they push.
