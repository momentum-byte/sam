Age Tracks
Two tracks. One season.
Same weekly themes and character curriculum across both tracks — coaching intensity and technical skills are age-adjusted.
Ages 4–7

Rally Starter

Introduction to movement, racquet sports fundamentals, and court awareness. Short rallies, big fun. Heavy emphasis on RESET routine and emotional regulation for early childhood.

Ages 8–11

Dink Maker

Real pickleball mechanics — dinks, volleys, serve & return, and mini match play. Character themes run deeper, with journaling, reflection, and peer accountability built in.

Programme Outcomes
What every child leaves with
Five measurable character outcomes — designed with Learning Fresh and tracked across the season by coaches and the LF framework.
🧘

Self-Control + Emotional Regulation

Calm body, calm words. The RESET routine becomes a daily tool kids carry off the court.

💪

Resilience

Bounce back after mistakes. "Next point mindset" — mistake = information, try again.

🤝

Teamwork + Respectful Communication

Partner drills and match play that build listening, encouragement, and accountability.

Fair Play + Honest Calls

Shared Language
Words every coach and child know by heart
The LF framework gives the programme a consistent vocabulary — used by PSC coaches, Learning Fresh facilitators, and taken home by kids.

"RESET"

Pause → hands to chest → 3 breaths → name the feeling → rejoin. Every coach uses it. Every child knows it. Every parent can use it at home.

"Next Point Mindset"

A mistake is information, not failure. Try again. This is the mental framework we build across every week of the season.
