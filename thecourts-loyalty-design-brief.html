01 — Design Objective

Make someone scan in 3 seconds.

This is not a loyalty programme campaign. This is a sign-up campaign. Every asset should answer "Why should I join right now?" before it explains how the programme works.

3seconds

The first impression must communicate:

  • What reward they can get right now
  • Where to scan to claim it
10seconds

Once they stop, they can understand:

  • Scan the QR code
  • Sign up in seconds
  • Earn points every visit
  • Redeem rewards across The Courts

Supporting Messages — Use across all assets

Join once. Unlock rewards across The Courts. Scan. Sign up. Earn. Redeem. No app. No card. Just scan and join. Earn every time you play, dine, train, or treat yourself. Rewards across sports, F&B, fitness, wellness, and beauty.

05 — QR Code Rules

The QR is never an afterthought.

Every asset lives or dies by whether someone scans. Treat the QR with the same weight as the headline.

🔲

Placeholder for Mockups

Use a placeholder QR code for all mockup submissions. Final QR code will be supplied by the client before production sign-off.

⬜

Size

QR must be large and comfortably scannable at the expected viewing distance for each format.

📐

Clear Space

Never place the QR too close to edges. Maintain generous clear space on all four sides.

🔗

Pair with CTA

Always label the QR with "Scan to Join" or "Scan Here" — never leave it floating without instruction.

🎯

Visual Connection

QR should be visually connected to the main CTA — not isolated in a corner as a secondary element.

📏

Scan Distance Test

For each format, confirm the QR can be comfortably scanned at realistic customer distance before finalising.

⚡

Contrast

High contrast on all backgrounds. No QR on busy imagery without a white or cream backing block.

06 — Visual Style

Premium lifestyle. Warm and human.

The Courts is not a gym chain or a corporate rewards scheme. It's a premium, community-rooted destination. The design should feel like it belongs in a boutique hotel lobby or an aspirational lifestyle magazine — not a mall flyer.

Typography Direction

Display / Headline

  • Strong, bold serif for headlines
  • Use weight to create hierarchy
  • Consider editorial serif accent where suitable
  • Generous leading, no crowding

Body / Supporting

  • Clean, modern sans-serif for body copy
  • Large, readable type for on-ground pieces
  • Minimal use of italic — reserve for tone moments
  • Never below 12pt for print pieces

✓ Do This

    • Premium lifestyle feel
    • Warm and inviting tone
    • High contrast, clean layouts
    • Reward-led visual hierarchy
    • Community-oriented imagery
    • Generous white space

✕ Avoid This

  • Too much body text on any asset
  • Tiny type or over-explanation
  • Generic retail discount poster look
  • Cartoonish or childish aesthetic
  • Corporate / cold visual language
  • QR code buried or treated as small
  • Over-explaining RM1 = 1 point mechanic
