Internal discussion

Continue conversation

No reply yet
Send follow-up
Follow up within a few business days if no response received
Still no response
Close out the lead — no further follow-up
End — lead closed
Phase 4 — Proposal & negotiation
Send proposal
BD team prepares and sends the partnership proposal to the client
Client reviews & responds
Client may come back with questions, feedback, or requests to negotiate
Discuss & find middle ground
BD team works through negotiations until both parties are aligned
📋  Throughout this phase, management (Mark & Dorcas) are kept in the loop on a weekly basis on all active leads and moving parts.
Phase 5 — Approval & sign-off
Final management approval
Mark & Dorcas give final sign-off to confirm and close the deal
Client signs partnership offer letter
Partnership is finalised and officially confirmed
