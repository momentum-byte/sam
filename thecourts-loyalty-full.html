The Concept

What Are We Building?

This is not just a points card. It is a marketing engine — a shared ecosystem where members earn and spend across all 7 tenants, and The Courts owns the intelligence layer that drives behaviour, cross-sells tenants, and reactivates cold members.

01

A Shared Ecosystem

One membership, seven tenants. Members earn points at PSC, spend them at Tanamerah, get rewarded at TRIBE. The Courts owns the portfolio-level data. Tenants benefit from each other's traffic without sharing sensitive data.

Community First
02

A Marketing Engine

Points are a vehicle to drive visits, not a cash-back scheme. The quarterly report tells us who's spending where, who's gone cold, and where we cross-sell. New tenants get allocated marketing spend here to drive trial.

Behaviour Driver
03

Start Simple, Scale Smart

Full automation and advanced tracking come later. The immediate goal: get members engaging, get tenants seeing redemptions within the first 30 days, and build confidence in the system before adding complexity.

The Welcome Boost

Why We Give 200 Points on Day One

A loyalty programme that feels inactive is worse than no programme at all. The welcome boost solves the cold-start problem — every new member can redeem something immediately.

200

🎂
Birthday Reward — Funded by The Courts
Every member gets a free birthday drink at Tanamerah Coffee — no points required. Data capture (birthday) is mandatory at sign-up. Low cost, high emotional impact, drives a venue visit.
⚡
Why 200 pts — Not More, Not Less
Enough to unlock a Tier 1 reward immediately — creates the first win. Not enough to skip to Tier 3 — keeps earning behaviour required. The member has to come back to progress.

Tenant Benefit: Day-1 Footfall
Every welcome boost redemption is a new customer through a tenant's door within the first week of joining. Tenants see proof of the programme working before the month is out.
🗓️
Points Expiry: 12 Months
Points expire 12 months from first earn. This creates urgency to redeem without feeling punitive. Communicated clearly at sign-up: "RM1 = 1 point. Easy to earn, good to redeem."

Member Lifecycle

How We Manage Every Member

Every member moves through four stages in their relationship with The Courts. Each stage has a different job — get them in, deepen their habits, catch them before they go cold, bring them back if they do.

Stage 1 — Welcome

First 30 Days

Trigger: Sign-up
  • 200-pt welcome boost gifted immediately
  • Birthday data captured at registration
  • First redemption encouraged within Week 1
  • Intro comms: explain how earning works, show the catalogue
  • Goal: one visit, one redemption, one habit formed
Stage 2 — Development

Month 1–3

Trigger: Regular activity detected
  • Cross-tenant nudges: "You're 100pts away from a TRIBE class"
  • Progress towards Tier 2 and 3 communicated
  • Spend-and-get promotions at specific tenants
  • New tenant introductions routed through programme
  • Goal: visit 2+ tenants per month
Stage 3 — Prevention

Early Churn Signal

Trigger: No activity for 3–4 weeks
  • Surprise & delight offer pushed to member
  • Cross-tenant insight used: "Still at NOA but left Pilates?"
  • Points balance reminder — "Don't let your points expire"
  • Targeted voucher for the tenant they last visited
  • Goal: one visit before full churn
Stage 4 — Reactivation

Cold Members

Trigger: No activity for 6+ weeks
  • Reactivation bonus points offer (e.g. +100pts to come back)
  • High-value bundle: visit 2 tenants, unlock special reward
  • Event-based reactivation: invite to Drive & Dink, EPIC, etc.
  • Last-chance expiry comms before points lapse
  • Goal: collect data even if they don't return — inform future campaigns

Business Model & Principles

The Rules We Don't Break

The programme only works long-term if it's financially sound for tenants and genuinely valuable for members. These principles keep both sides of that equation healthy.

01

The Courts Owns the Data

Individual tenants do not share data with each other. The Courts sees the full portfolio view — who visits where, cross-tenant patterns, cold member signals. This is the programme's most valuable asset and must be protected.

02

Vouchers Over Cash — For Now

Points unlock vouchers and experiences in Phase 1. This protects tenant margins, creates conditional spend behaviour, and builds the programme's value perception before we explore more flexible redemption options down the road.

03

Tenants Don't Loss-Make on BAU

Year-long (BAU) vouchers must be margin-positive or neutral for each tenant. Loss-making vouchers are only permitted when there is a specific, time-bound acquisition objective — not as a permanent catalogue item.

04

The Programme Earns Its Keep

Every new tenant added to The Courts ecosystem gets a marketing spend allocation through the loyalty programme. This makes the programme a direct revenue argument for Mark when onboarding new tenants — the programme pays for itself.

05

Quarterly Reports Are Non-Negotiable

Every 3 months: average spend per member, which tenants are being cross-sold into, cold member %, redemption rates. This report is the tool that keeps tenants engaged and shows the programme's commercial value to the ecosystem.

06

Start Simple, Always

Full automation, advanced segmentation, and tier upgrades come later. What matters now: get members in, get them redeeming, and get tenants seeing results within 30 days. Complexity is earned, not assumed.
